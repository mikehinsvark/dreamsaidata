GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity goes a long way.

Know what to expect before you explore joining.

Do I need to upload a dataset to join?

No dataset upload is requested on this landing page. Joining takes you to DataSupply; review any requirements there before sharing information.

Is the $50,000–$100,000 estimate guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate of average potential earnings per business, not a promise or offer. Eligibility, buyer demand, fit, rights, and final terms may affect the outcome, and an opportunity may not be available.

Does clicking “Join the network” confirm a partnership?

No. Opening the signup page does not confirm a partnership. You decide whether to consider any opportunity or agreement.

What should be reviewed before any data is shared?

Confirm the receiving buyer, permitted uses, scope, confidentiality and security safeguards, privacy requirements, and written agreement terms. Get advice from your own qualified legal adviser where appropriate.
